The primary audience is already decided: **brands and distributors first, focused on partnership enquiries**. The following decisions do not prevent preparing the design or content structure, but affect the release content and functioning contact journey.

| Priority | Decision | Proposed default | Confirmation source |
|---|---|---|---|

## Publication rules for future implementation

Each quantitative claim should carry a source, page/slide, reporting date, metric definition, geography/product scope, owner and publication status. A customer logo also needs a relationship type and permission record. If a claim is not cleared for the page's intended audience, the build should omit it rather than substitute a made-up number.

Keep this register, the supplied documents and extracted operational data outside public web assets. They contain commercial proposals and identifiable operational records. Public case studies should use the approved subset only.
